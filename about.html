Our Legacy

Crafting India's most distinguished luxury residences with an uncompromising commitment to quality, heritage, and architectural excellence.

The Genesis (2009)

Aurelian Estates was founded in 2009 in Bengaluru with a singular, unyielding vision: to construct homes that transcend generations. At a time when the real estate landscape was consumed by mass scale and rapid expansion, we consciously chose the path of absolute restraint.

Our very first project, Aurelian Genesis, was situated in the elite enclave of Sadashivanagar. It was a boutique collection of just four masterfully crafted villas. We poured everything into those four homes — sourcing the finest Italian marble, collaborating with master artisans, and obsessing over every millimeter of space and light.

Today, with a portfolio spanning the nation's most coveted zip codes, our philosophy remains exactly the same. We do not build to fill space; we build to define it.
